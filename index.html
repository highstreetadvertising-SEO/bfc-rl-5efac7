The number of sessions required depends on the nature and severity of your condition, your age, your overall health, and how you respond to treatment. A qualified Puyallup chiropractor will provide a personalized treatment plan with realistic timelines during your initial evaluation.

5. Can chiropractic care help with headaches and migraines?

Yes, chiropractic adjustments — particularly cervical spine manipulation — have shown significant effectiveness in reducing the frequency and severity of tension headaches and some forms of migraines. Many patients in Puyallup seek chiropractic care specifically for headache relief and report meaningful improvements after consistent treatment.

6. What should I expect during my first chiropractic appointment?

Your first visit will typically include a comprehensive health history intake, a physical examination, postural assessment, and neurological and orthopedic testing as needed. The chiropractor will then discuss their findings, diagnosis, and recommended treatment plan. Some clinics may perform an adjustment on the first visit; others schedule it for a follow-up appointment once they've fully reviewed your case.

Yes, chiropractic care is generally safe during pregnancy when performed by a practitioner trained in prenatal techniques. Absolutely. Whiplash, soft tissue injuries, and spinal misalignments are common results of motor vehicle accidents, and chiropractic care is one of the most effective treatments for these conditions. 10. How do I know if I need a chiropractor or a physical therapist?

Both chiropractic care and physical therapy address musculoskeletal health, but they approach it differently. Chiropractors focus primarily on spinal alignment and nervous system function through adjustments, while physical therapists emphasize strengthening exercises and movement rehabilitation. Many patients benefit from both, and quality clinics like Berry Family Chiropractic can help guide you toward the right type of care or coordinate with physical therapy providers as needed.
